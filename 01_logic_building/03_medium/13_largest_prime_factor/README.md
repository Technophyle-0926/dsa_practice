# 13. Largest Prime factor

**Category:** Logic Building → Medium

## Problem

Given a positive integer n ( 1 <= n <= 10^15). Find the largest prime factor of a number.

```
Input: 6
Output: 3
Explanation Prime factor are 2 and 3. Largest of them is 3.

Input: 15
Output: 5
Explanation: Prime factors are 3 and 5. The largest of them is 5.
```

```
Input: 28
Output: 7
Explanation: Prime factors are 2 and 7. The largest of them is 7.
```
